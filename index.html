Summary

Vencer Group is a Calgary managed IT provider that reviews each of its 10–20 clients every quarter, showing what they got for their money and what needs attention next. The evidence for those reviews sits in about a dozen vendor platforms that do not share data, so each review took hours of logging in and copying numbers by hand. We built the Vencer Operations Dashboard, an internal web application that collects this data on a schedule, keeps every reading, and rates each client Healthy, Watch or At risk across six categories. Staff can see which client needs attention from one screen, open the figures behind any rating, and generate a draft quarterly review that a person checks and approves before exporting it to Word.

The problem

About a dozen platforms that don't share data, copied by hand for hours per client. Figures came out differently each quarter, so reviews were often skipped.
