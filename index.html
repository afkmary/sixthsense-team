About Vencer Group

Vencer Group is a Calgary managed IT provider. Companies pay Vencer to run their IT, from laptops and servers to networks, email security and backups.

Every quarter, Vencer sits down with each client to show what they got for their money and what needs attention next.

How it works

Getting one client's quarterly numbers used to mean logging into about a dozen vendor platforms and copying figures by hand. Now the system does the gathering, and people do the checking.

  1. 01

    Connect

    Reads about a dozen vendor platforms through their APIs on a schedule. Read-only, always.

  2. 02

    Store

    Every reading is kept and never overwritten, so any past report can be rebuilt exactly as it was.

  3. 03

    Calculate

    Works out what a review needs: response times, patching, backup success, MFA coverage.

  4. 04

    Draft

    A Qwen model on Vencer's own server writes the wording. It's handed the numbers, so it can't invent one.

  5. 05

    Review

    Staff see every client's health on one screen, then check, edit and approve each quarterly report.
